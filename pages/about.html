Meet the Founder
Dr. Meenakshi K S
Body · Mind · Soul Coach & Wellness Guide

I am Dr. Meenakshi K S, a Body–Mind–Soul coach, healer, and wellness guide with over 25 years of experience in the field of yoga and holistic healing. My life's purpose is to empower individuals to rise into their highest potential — physically, emotionally, and spiritually.

My journey began as a passionate yoga practitioner, and over the years, it expanded into deeper healing sciences. I am an internationally certified Sound Therapist, Marma Therapist, Pranic Healer, Reiki Grand Master, NLP Practitioner, Meditation and Pranayama Coach, and a certified Nutritionist and Life Coach.

"My mission is simple yet profound: to touch a million lives through the power of awareness, breath, movement, and healing."

As the founder of Aarohana Yoga, I have guided thousands of people through yoga, therapeutic practices, emotional healing, and spiritual growth. My approach combines traditional yogic wisdom with powerful energy-healing techniques to create deep, lasting transformation.

Whether it is helping someone improve flexibility, release emotional blockages, manage stress, or reconnect with themselves — I believe in meeting every person with compassion, understanding, and unwavering support.

Our Philosophy

Healing as an
ascent

We believe healing is an ascent — a gentle rise from chaos to clarity, from imbalance to inner harmony. Through our holistic practices, we invite you to slow down, reconnect with yourself, and experience the sacred silence within.

At Aarohana, every breath is a step upward — toward awareness, wholeness, and light. Each program is thoughtfully crafted to awaken awareness, restore energy, and nurture peace from within.

"True wellness is not just physical fitness, but a journey of self-discovery, inner balance, and conscious living."
