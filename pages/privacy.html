Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026  ·  Aarohana Yoga, Bengaluru

At Aarohana Yoga, your privacy matters to us deeply. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you use our website (aarohanayoga.com), how we use it, and the choices you have. We keep things simple — we are a small yoga studio, not a data company.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following when you interact with our website or contact us directly:

  • Contact form submissions — your name, email address, phone number, and any message you send us through the booking or enquiry form.
  • WhatsApp messages — if you reach us via our WhatsApp link, your messages are subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy.
  • Usage data — basic analytics such as pages visited and browser type, collected anonymously through tools like GitHub Pages hosting logs. We do not use Google Analytics or other tracking scripts.

We do not collect payment information on this website. Any payments are handled in person or through third-party apps directly.

2. How We Use Your Information

Any information you share with us is used solely to:

  • Respond to your enquiries or booking requests
  • Send you information about upcoming events or workshops you've expressed interest in
  • Improve our website and services based on how people use it

We will never sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Cookies

This website does not use cookies for tracking or advertising. The only cookies that may appear are those set by third-party embeds such as the Google Maps iframe in our Contact section — these are governed by Google's own privacy policy.

4. Third-Party Services

Our website links to or embeds the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Google Maps — for our studio location embed
  • WhatsApp — for direct messaging via our chat links
  • Instagram & Facebook — links to our social profiles
  • Google Fonts — for typography (Cormorant Garamond, Jost)
  • GitHub Pages — our hosting platform

5. Data Retention

We retain contact form submissions only as long as necessary to respond to your enquiry. If you'd like us to delete any information you've shared with us, simply email us and we will do so promptly.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Opt out of any communications from us at any time

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.

7. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Our Kids Summer Camp enquiries are handled through parent or guardian contact only.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review it periodically.
